# Medicare Secondary Payer Lien Resolution

Resolve Medicare conditional-payment obligations accurately while protecting settlement value and statutory compliance.

**Primary buyer:** Law firms, self-insured employers, and claims administrators. **Evidence:** eligibility, conditional payments, claims, liability events, settlement terms, procurement costs, demand letters, disputes, and reimbursements.

Full local application built with React, Vite, Express, PostgreSQL, and OpenRouter. Includes 15 domain-specific capabilities, 105 custom AI workbench fields, three scenario-fill controls per feature, operational registers, workflow transitions, analytics, professional AI decision briefs, audit history, and at least 15 PostgreSQL records per capability.

## Domain capabilities

- Beneficiary eligibility verification
- Liability event registry
- Conditional payment ingestion
- Claim relatedness review
- Unrelated claim dispute
- Procurement cost allocation
- Demand amount calculation
- Settlement reporting control
- Section 111 evidence
- Final demand tracking
- Appeal waiver workflow
- Payment reconciliation
- Release letter evidence
- Settlement reserve forecast
- Case outcome analytics
